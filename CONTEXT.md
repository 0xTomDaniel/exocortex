# Exo planning and memory

Exo preserves commitments and supporting knowledge across conversation and review.

## Language

**Commitment**: An outcome accepted as work to pursue; scheduling it later does
not release it.

**Someday**: A preserved possibility that is not a current commitment.
_Avoid_: Later, Incubating as lifecycle states

**Done**: The intended outcome was achieved.
_Avoid_: Reviewed, Closed as synonyms for completion

**Closed**: A commitment ended without completion, or a document or ongoing
practice was retired, with a recorded reason.

**Ended period**: A period whose time window elapsed; its review and unfinished
commitments may remain open.

**Reminder**: A dated attention hook on any note, independent of whether that
note represents a task.
