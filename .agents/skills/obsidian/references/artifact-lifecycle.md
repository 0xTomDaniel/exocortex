# Artifact lifecycle

- Editable instruction sources live in the canonical skill directories and AGENTS.md. Provider views and `.skill` archives are generated exports; regenerate after source checks instead of editing them.
- Notes are memory/state/history. Organize by stable note class and represent alternate dimensions with links/properties. Preserve user wording and historical decisions.
- Captured media needs a companion source note. Large evidence collections can legitimately contain raw snapshots; record their owner, provenance, and retention purpose before proposing deletion.
- Hash-bound assessment banks/runtime snapshots belong to their versioned evidence contract. Apparent duplication alone is not grounds to prune them.
- Browser profiles, caches, logs, audio-tool backups and process files are local runtime material. Keep them out of source packages and repository imports. Retention/deletion requires an identified owner and an explicit retention decision.
- Repair operational navigation first. Taxonomy links and intentionally unresolved conceptual references do not require empty stub notes. Do not manufacture missing historical period records.
- Generate exports from validated sources, exclude caches/runtime files, and compare archive members to source bytes. Keep a digest manifest alongside distributed exports.
