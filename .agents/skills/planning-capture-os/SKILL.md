# Planning Capture OS

Use this skill when the job is to take raw captures and route them into the right durable homes.

## Core model

- Treat the inbox as a **temporary capture layer**, not storage.
- Clarify each item by its nature before deciding where it belongs.
- Do not turn non-commitments into tasks.
- Keep queues finite and energy-aware.
- Treat this skill and its references as the **canonical operational source** for capture/inbox routing unless explicitly superseded.

## Default workflow

1. **Identify the capture job**
   - Distinguish between:
     - raw tab dump
     - mixed inbox processing
     - queue triage
     - reference-note creation
     - discard / closure cleanup

2. **Read the right reference**
   - Read `references/inbox-routing.md` for the canonical classify-and-route model.
   - If routing creates or updates tasks, read `planning-task-os` for task-schema semantics.
   - If routing happens inside a day-start / review ritual, also read `planning-rhythm-os` for cadence context.

3. **Route, then normalize**
   - Convert kept items into the right queue/note/task/reference home.
   - Preserve project scope and do not collapse unrelated items into a nearby project just because it exists.

## Guardrails

- Do not create one task per tab by default.
- Do not keep one giant permanent mixed backlog.
- Do not assume everything belongs in a queue.
- Do not preserve low-value items just because they were once open.
